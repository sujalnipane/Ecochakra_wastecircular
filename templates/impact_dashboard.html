🏙️ Human & Slum Community Impact

Air Pollution

Open waste burning can release harmful smoke and particulate matter.

Water Contamination

Improper waste disposal can contaminate local water sources.

Vector-Borne Disease Risk

Accumulated waste and stagnant water can create conditions favourable to mosquitoes and other vectors.

Flooding & Waterlogging

Plastic and solid waste can obstruct drainage systems, increasing local flooding risk.
